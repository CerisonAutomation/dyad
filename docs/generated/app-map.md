## Conversion boundaries

1. Renderer: model/source picker, settings, onboarding, banners, budget/quota dialogs, preview/annotation gates.
2. Routing: settings normalization, persisted per-chat selection, inference factory, model catalog and automatic/fallback candidates.
3. Agents/tools: root Agent2, Explorer/Implementer/testing/history subagents, safety reviewer, shell/MCP consents, engine-only tools. Preserve safety guards.
4. IPC/services: billing admissions, credits, quota, checkout/deep links, cloud catalogs, uploads, help and auxiliary inference.
5. Preview/runtime: Electron sessions, iframe/webview, annotator, screenshot, app lifecycle. BYOK must not gate local features behind credit balance.
6. Compatibility: migrate old settings explicitly; do not silently bill a different provider. Claude/ChatGPT provider connections are not Dyad subscriptions.
7. Preserve license/copyright/attribution; historical tests and migration keys are distinct from visible product UI. Do not claim legally required notices can be erased.
